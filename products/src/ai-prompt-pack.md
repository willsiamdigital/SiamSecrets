# Read this first

This pack works with any AI text or music tool (Suno, Udio, ChatGPT, Claude, and similar). Each prompt is a fill-in-the-blank template. Replace the **[brackets]** with your own details.

**The formula for a great music prompt**

> **Genre + Mood + Tempo + Instruments + Vocal style + Story/Theme**

The more specific you are, the less generic the output.

**Important:** Always read your platform's terms for commercial use and check what rights you hold over AI-generated audio. Write your own lyrics and melody when you want to own the work outright.

# Bonus: The 4-Step Iteration Loop

1. **Generate** 3 variations.
2. **Pick** the one with the strongest hook.
3. **Rewrite** the weakest lines yourself. This is where your voice enters.
4. **Regenerate** only the sections that are weak, not the whole song.
