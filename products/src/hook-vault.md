# How to use this vault

A hook is the part of the song people remember. This vault gives you 120 hook ideas, each with a **title**, an **angle** (the emotional spin), and a **first line** to get you writing in under a minute.

**Three ways to use it**

1. **Steal the angle, not the words.** Keep the emotional situation, change the details to your life.
2. **Flip it.** Take any hook and write the opposite (a breakup hook becomes a "we made it" hook).
3. **Stack two.** Combine the title from one with the angle from another.

**Rule of thumb:** a strong hook is short (2 to 7 words), sings easily on a repeated melody, and says one clear feeling.

# Bonus: 5 Hook-Writing Exercises

1. **The 10-Word Rule.** Write your hook as a text message in under 10 words.
2. **Say It Out Loud.** If you can't say it in one breath, shorten it.
3. **Change the Verb.** Rewrite the hook with 3 different verbs. Pick the sharpest.
4. **Rhythm First.** Tap the rhythm of the hook on your desk before choosing words.
5. **The Taxi Test.** Explain the song to a stranger in one sentence. That sentence is probably your hook.
