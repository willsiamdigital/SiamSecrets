# Welcome

Most songs never get finished. This workbook gives you **20 to 40 minutes a day for 7 days** and a clear job each day. By Day 7 you will have a complete demo-ready song.

**What you need:** a pen or notes app, your phone's voice recorder, and any instrument (or just your voice).

**Rules**

- Finish each day, even if it is rough.
- No editing before Day 6.
- Record everything on your phone.

---

# Day 1: Find the Idea (20 min)

**Goal:** Pick one song idea.

1. Write 10 things you felt this week (one line each).
2. Circle the 3 strongest.
3. For each, answer: *What happened? Who was there? What did I want to say but didn't?*
4. Choose one. Write it as a single sentence:

> This song is about ______ and the feeling is ______.

**Checklist:** [ ] 10 feelings [ ] 3 circled [ ] 1 sentence

---

# Day 2: Write the Hook (30 min)

**Goal:** Create your title and chorus idea.

1. Write 20 possible titles (2 to 7 words). Do not judge.
2. Pick the one you can imagine repeated.
3. Speak the title in 5 different rhythms. Record the one that sticks.
4. Hum 3 melodies for it. Record the best.

**Hook test**

- [ ] Short enough to sing in one breath
- [ ] One clear emotion
- [ ] Repeating it doesn't get boring

---

# Day 3: Build the Chords (30 min)

**Goal:** Pick a progression and a feel.

**Common progressions (in any major key)**

| Feel | Progression (Roman numerals) | In C major |
|------|------------------------------|------------|
| Uplifting | I – V – vi – IV | C – G – Am – F |
| Nostalgic | vi – IV – I – V | Am – F – C – G |
| Emotional | vi – V – IV – V | Am – G – F – G |
| Classic rock | I – IV – V – IV | C – F – G – F |
| Smooth/Jazzy | ii – V – I – vi | Dm – G – C – Am |
| Dark | i – VI – III – VII | Am – F – C – G (minor feel) |

1. Choose one progression for the chorus.
2. Choose a different one (or the same with a new rhythm) for the verse.
3. Play it for 5 minutes and sing your hook over it.
4. Record a 1-minute loop.

---

# Day 4: Verse 1 and Verse 2 (40 min)

**Goal:** Tell the story.

**Verse 1 = Setting.** Where are we? Who is here? Use 3 concrete details (objects, sounds, places).

**Verse 2 = Change.** What changed or got worse? Add one detail that surprises.

**Detail prompts**

- What was on the table / floor / radio?
- What time was it?
- What did they say exactly?
- What did you do with your hands?

**Rhyme scheme options:** ABAB, AABB, ABCB (easiest and most natural: lines 2 and 4 rhyme).

**Checklist:** [ ] Verse 1 (4 to 8 lines) [ ] Verse 2 (4 to 8 lines) [ ] Recorded rough take

---

# Day 5: Pre-Chorus and Bridge (30 min)

**Goal:** Add lift and a twist.

**Pre-chorus:** 2 to 4 lines that build tension. Raise the melody, shorten the lines, or end on an unresolved chord.

**Bridge:** Change one thing: perspective, chord, rhythm, or time. Say the thing you haven't said yet.

**Bridge starters**

- "But what if..."
- "Years from now..."
- "I never told you..."
- "If I could go back..."

---

# Day 6: Edit (30 min)

**Goal:** Make it better, not longer.

**The 6-pass edit**

1. **Cut** any line that doesn't serve the title.
2. **Swap** abstract words for concrete ones ("sadness" becomes "the dishes still in the sink").
3. **Check** rhythm: read aloud and mark stressed syllables.
4. **Check** rhyme: remove forced rhymes. Near rhymes are fine.
5. **Check** perspective: stay consistent (I/you/we).
6. **Check** the first line and last line. They carry the most weight.

---

# Day 7: Record and Finish (40 min)

**Goal:** Record a complete demo.

1. Warm up your voice for 5 minutes.
2. Record the whole song in one take. Don't stop for mistakes.
3. Record a second take if you want.
4. Name the file: `YYYY-MM-DD_Title_Demo`.
5. Share it with one person.

**You finished a song.** Repeat with a new idea.

# Appendix A: Song Structure Templates

| Structure | Use for |
|-----------|---------|
| Verse – Chorus – Verse – Chorus – Bridge – Chorus | Most pop/rock/country |
| Verse – Verse – Bridge – Verse | Folk/storytelling |
| Intro – Verse – Pre – Chorus – Verse – Pre – Chorus – Drop – Chorus | Electronic/dance pop |
| Hook – Verse – Hook – Verse – Hook | Hip-hop |

# Appendix C: Quick Rhyme Help

- **Perfect:** day/stay, night/light
- **Near:** home/alone, time/mine, heart/dark
- **Multisyllable:** remember/December, hesitate/celebrate
- **Internal:** put a rhyme in the middle of a line for energy.
